# Dragon Fantasy City — Complete Card Guide

Version 0.1.3 contains 46 original Jokers. Rarity is a design hierarchy, not just a price label: **Legendaries establish permanent engines, Rares redirect the run, and Uncommons provide readable combo pieces.**

## Legendary (4)

| # | Joker | Effect | Gameplay and visual intent |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Smaug, the Red Dragon | Every $7 actively spent permanently adds X0.20 Mult. | Turns spending from a loss into long-term investment. Gold, flame, and greed make the engine immediately legible. |
| 2 | Asabith, the Venom Dragon | For every 3 playing cards destroyed, all played cards permanently gain one retrigger. | The ultimate destruction engine. Poison mist, broken cards, and claws frame sacrifice as fuel for an increasingly violent payoff. |
| 3 | Gorn, the Bone Dragon | After every played hand, adds a random-suit Red Seal Steel King directly to the current hand. | One creation connects Steel, Seals, Kings, added-card triggers, and hand economy. Bone and cold lightning suggest dead material reforged into a weapon. |
| 4 | Chinese Dragon | Whenever another non-Common Joker produces an effective trigger, permanently gains +1 Mult. | Rewards a whole board of interacting Jokers instead of one carry. The dragon pearl represents power gathered from many sources. |

## Rare (14)

| # | Joker | Effect | Gameplay and visual intent |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 5 | Captain Cook | The first time each hand type is played during a Blind, gain $3. Repeating a hand type ends the reward for that Blind. | A voyage that cannot retrace its route. Compass and path motifs mirror deliberate hand sequencing. |
| 6 | Shadow Warrior | The ranks of played black cards that do not score become permanent Chips. | Converts deliberate dead weight into training. Black suits and negative space make the hidden-value idea visible. |
| 7 | Phantom Ninja | At the end of each Blind, has a 1-in-3 chance to make another Joker Negative. | A rare slot-breaking jackpot. The character nearly disappears into darkness, echoing a capacity that appears from nowhere. |
| 8 | Mad Treant | Every other effective Joker triggers one additional time. It cannot retrigger itself or another copy. | A master switch for trigger engines, with explicit recursion boundaries. Branches resemble a network carrying effects between Jokers. |
| 9 | T-800 | The first card in the first discard of each Blind is destroyed and returns to hand as Steel. | Turns termination into reconstruction. Metal anatomy and a red scan line visualize mechanical conversion. |
| 10 | Sun Wukong | Each hand has a 1-in-3 chance to turn all scoring cards into random Enhancements. | Seventy-two transformations as permanent deck mutation. Clouds and shifting color blocks communicate explosive uncertainty. |
| 11 | Bull Demon King | Each hand has a 1-in-3 chance to destroy its scoring cards and permanently add their rank values to Mult. | A sacrifice engine with a frightening ceiling. Horns, lava, and crushed cards show the bargain before the text is read. |
| 12 | Great White Shark | Each hand has a 1-in-4 chance to destroy all played cards and create two Planet cards. | The shark devours a hand and spits out growth. Cards in the jaws keep the destructive target visually explicit. |
| 13 | Cannon Mark | Each destroyed Stone Card permanently adds X0.5 Mult, starting at X1. | Stones become ammunition. Barrel, rubble, and one impact line compress the entire build instruction into a symbol. |
| 14 | Spider Witch | The first playing-card retrigger in each hand creates one Tarot card. | Converts a retrigger web into consumable economy. Her cards and threads tell the same “catch and weave” story. |
| 15 | Striped Python | Every scoring card in a Straight retriggers twice. | A snake body is already a continuous sequence, making art and rule structurally identical. |
| 16 | The Great Wall | Whenever exactly five cards are played—regardless of how many score—permanently gains +8 Chips. | Every five-card play lays another brick. Repeated battlements make accumulation the visual subject. |
| 17 | Tokamak | Doubles the Blind requirement; on entering the Blind, doubles money up to $40. | A high-energy bargain that raises both fuel and containment pressure. The hot core is powerful only because the ring restrains it. |
| 18 | Silver Walker | After the first paid reroll in each shop, makes one random Booster free. The player opens it normally. | Converts rerolling into a hunt. Preserving the normal click flow delivers the reward without bypassing pack state transitions. |

## Uncommon (28)

| # | Joker | Effect | Gameplay and visual intent |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 19 | Genghis Khan | Reading left to right, each scoring card higher than the previous one gives +7 Mult. | Increasing ranks become a forward conquest; order is part of the puzzle. |
| 20 | Melatonin | Adds two face-down cards to each hand. If neither is played, gain X1.40 Mult. | A restraint reward built around the unknown. Moon plus two dark cards is enough to carry the concept. |
| 21 | Dwarf Miner | Discard exactly one Stone Card to destroy it and gain $4. | Stone becomes ore and a single discard becomes precise extraction. |
| 22 | Oak Shield | After each discard, draw up to two cards back from the discard pile. | Makes discarding partially recoverable; a shield surrounding a return arrow says it all. |
| 23 | First Blood | The first hand of each Blind destroys its first scoring card and uses that rank as Mult for the Blind. | The opening casualty becomes fighting spirit. A single red cut focuses the one-use commitment. |
| 24 | White Elephant | Shop items cost half, but rerolls cost twice as much. | A clean economic trade: buy what is offered instead of searching. Elephant and scales communicate heavy but fair value. |
| 25 | Wild Pear Tree | A Straight Flush gains +50 Chips. | A direct, dependable reward for a difficult hand type, represented by one tree and a suited sequence. |
| 26 | Suspect X | At each Blind, two random cards are flipped; one hidden choice contributes twice its rank as Mult. | An incomplete-information deduction problem. Two suspects and a fork preserve uncertainty. |
| 27 | Swamp Crocodile | At the end of the Blind, destroys the lowest-ranked card in hand and pays dollars equal to its rank. | Cleans the bottom of the deck for a modest return. The lowest card visibly sinks toward the jaws. |
| 28 | Drifting Iceberg | Stores base Chips from unscored played cards. On the final hand, pays the stored Chips and grants one extra hand. | Early value hides below the waterline and the full mass appears only at the end. |
| 29 | Gumpy, the Ugly Giant | If five cards are played but only one or two score, gain +30 Mult. | Rewards a deliberately clumsy hand structure: a huge body supported by very few useful cards. |
| 30 | Onion Knight | Gain X0.25 Mult for each discard remaining. | Every saved discard is another layer of armor. |
| 31 | Zombie Shaun | Twice the highest rank discarded during the current Blind is added as Mult. | The largest discarded card comes back from the dead as damage. |
| 32 | Rapper Hawk | The second and fifth scoring cards each retrigger once. | Fixed beats create two accents; a 2/5 rhythm and waveform replace unnecessary character detail. |
| 33 | Ethan, Fund Manager | Pay $5 on selecting a Blind. Winning in one hand returns $12; winning in two returns $5. | Invest first, then prove execution speed. Coins and a steep return line frame a short, pressured trade. |
| 34 | Preacher | Scoring 6s and 9s each gain X2 Mult. | The rotational mirror of 6 and 9 is a ready-made icon and a clear deck-building target. |
| 35 | Road Roller | On the final hand, playing both the current highest and lowest ranks creates a Spectral card afterward. | Forces both ends of the deck onto one road; compressing extremes produces rare material. |
| 36 | Gangster Goon | Hearts and Diamonds give +4 Mult; Clubs and Spades give +30 Chips. | Red and black factions own different scoring layers, shown as a two-color split. |
| 37 | Crab Catcher | After the first hand of each Blind, copies its highest-ranked face card back into hand. | A claw catches the biggest face card and pulls it back for another use. |
| 38 | Javelin Thrower | Gain +10 Chips for each rank of difference between the first and last scoring cards. J/Q/K=10, A=11. | The distance between endpoints becomes the javelin's flight. |
| 39 | Camellia Lady | At the end of the Blind, destroys the leftmost card in hand and copies the rightmost card. | A stage-like exchange: the left side withers and the right side blooms. |
| 40 | Strong Sailor | Every consumable used adds +2 Chips to every scoring card in the next hand; stacks until that hand resolves. | Consumables become a short burst of strength, meant to be stockpiled before a decisive play. |
| 41 | Serotonin | On entering a Blind, equally likely to gain $10, a Spectral card, or a Tarot card. | A three-part reward wheel keeps the anticipation readable and cheerful. |
| 42 | Troubled Werther | Starts at X3 Mult; loses X0.05 for every card played, never below X1. | Great power under constant emotional erosion, pushing the player to win quickly. |
| 43 | Hacker | After the first paid reroll in each shop, makes one random item cost $0. | A reroll opens one pricing exploit; a broken tag and green terminal icon tell the story. |
| 44 | Rubber Head | Gains twice the total base Chips of all cards left unplayed in hand. | Cards kept in hand lose their shapes but retain their numerical value, rewarding small scoring hands. |
| 45 | Clockwork Orange | Cards newly added to the deck score at X1.5 and retrigger once. | New cards receive clockwork machinery, unifying every card-generation route into one engine. |
| 46 | Django | The first exactly two-card hand of each Blind randomly destroys one card after scoring and transfers its rank as permanent bonus Chips to the survivor. | A two-card duel: one must fall and the winner inherits the loser. Two opposing silhouettes keep the concept stark. |

## Suggested engines

- **Destruction**: Asabith + Bull Demon King / Great White Shark / Dwarf Miner / Django.
- **Added cards**: Gorn + Clockwork Orange. Gorn's Red Seal Steel King enters the current hand and uses the standard playing-card-added flow.
- **Retriggers**: Mad Treant + Spider Witch + Striped Python. Watch available consumable slots.
- **Economy**: Smaug + Captain Cook / Ethan / Hacker / Silver Walker.
- **Small hands**: Rubber Head + Ugly Giant + Django, extracting value from cards deliberately left out of scoring.
